# Kimi K2.7 Handoff — GamePilot Website Translation

## Goal

Translate the GamePilot desktop app into a marketing / landing website for a separate project. The website should communicate the core philosophy, key features, and recent improvements from v1.7.0.

## Core positioning

GamePilot is **not** a launcher.

GamePilot is a **local-first decision-making layer** for your game library:
- It tells you what to play based on your mood, time, and taste.
- It builds a private gaming identity from your actual play data.
- It surfaces smart shelves and taste clusters so you stop scrolling and start playing.

Differentiator vs Playnite: Playnite is a library manager. GamePilot is a personal gaming recommendation engine.

## Key messages to include

1. **Your library. Your stats. Your machine.**
2. **Local-first, private, no cloud, no telemetry.**
3. **Identity, Insights, and Smart Shelves** are the heart of the app.
4. **Playtime tracking even when Steam is offline.**
5. **Perfect Play recommendations** based on mood, genre, and available time.

## Features to highlight (v1.7.0)

- **Gaming Identity** — built from real playtime, top genre, game count, and signature games.
- **Taste Clusters** — emergent patterns like Souls-like Specialist, Run Chaser, Precision Platformer.
- **Smart Shelves** — auto-curated collections: Continue This, Backlog Rediscovery, Comfort Picks, Hidden Gems, Deep Dives, One More Run, Quick Fix, Mood Match, Abandoned Early, Recently Completed.
- **Habit Insights** — peak play time, session trends, mood distribution, library health, time-of-day heatmap.
- **Year in Review** — available every day, not just once a year.
- **Rewards** — view unlocked card styles, themes, layouts, and progression rewards.
- **Performance Cockpit** — Labs feature for hardware compatibility and bottleneck analysis.
- **Local-first backup / restore** — your data stays on your machine.
